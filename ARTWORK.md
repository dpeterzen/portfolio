# City-window wallpaper artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These exterior city views show a tiny laptop user through a cozy bedroom window. Each scene retains the theme’s restrained tonal palette and pixel-art style.

## Dune — desert city

`dist/assets/dune-city-window.png`

Create a brand-new OUTDOOR EXTERIOR wide 16:9 pixel-art desktop wallpaper. Cozy futuristic desert CITY at night, viewed from a distant rooftop across several streets. See the COMPLETE FACADES and roofs of multiple blocky adobe apartment buildings, quiet alleys, small antennae, a few lanterns and warm windows, distant flat mesas. Camera is OUTSIDE in open air looking at the buildings, never inside a room. In ONE SMALL bedroom window on a building at 78% across and 60% down, a tiny person is sitting at a LAPTOP. This window is only 6% of the image width and 8% of its height. The person is an extremely tiny silhouette about 2% of total image height. Through the window you can just make out the laptop rectangle, desk, lamp, curtains and edge of a bed: a cozy occupied room amid a quiet city. Architecture and city dominate, the bedroom is a small detail to discover. Strong low-fi EARLY-1990s PIXEL GAME art: chunky square pixels, flat geometric color clusters, 240x135 game-resolution look enlarged nearest-neighbor. Strictly restrained palette of 8 SOLID colors in dark brown, muted taupe, dusty sand, with a tiny pale amber window accent. NO gradients, NO texture, NO noise, NO intricate brick detail, NO realism, NO painterly shading or soft glow. Buildings must be clearly readable simple shapes with enough detail for doors, rooflines, windows. Calm dark negative space across left third and upper sky for desktop icons and windows. Cozy, quiet, distant. No text, logos, interface, lettering, watermark.
