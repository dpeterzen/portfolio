# Environment artwork

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. The original PNGs were converted to JPEG for delivery; these assets are included in the repository.

## Dune

`dist/assets/dune.jpg`

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an ultrawide cinematic desktop wallpaper for a quiet futuristic western desert personal portfolio interface. Painterly high-end science fiction environment concept art with rich tactile fine grain. Massive sun-bleached beige sandstone mesas and rolling dunes, a tiny weathered retrofuturist outpost with a slender radio mast on far right third, a few warm red indicator lights, distant pale moon in a hazy desaturated sage-grey sky. Wind-scoured desert, dusty faded olive, sand, bone, muted rust. Serene, lonely, lived-in, future frontier, no neon metropolis. Strong composition: horizon just above center, huge breathtaking sky, sweeping dune foreground provides calm uncluttered negative space for UI folders. Soft late-afternoon overcast light, filmic atmospheric depth, very restrained saturation. Wide 16:9 landscape. Environment ONLY: no typography, text, borders, logos, icons, UI, characters, watermarks.

## Keep

`dist/assets/keep.jpg`

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an ultrawide cinematic desktop wallpaper for a mysterious mythical knight castle archive interface. A distant ancient dark medieval castle with narrow turrets emerges from mist at center-right, set on a rocky promontory above a still lake, long slender stone bridge entering from lower right. A faint warm light in one distant lancet window. Large pale moon veiled by clouds high right. Foreground moor and black trees fading into smoke, ancient solemn Arthurian fantasy, no people. Painterly romantic gothic oil painting mixed with textured cinematic concept art, nuanced blue-charcoal, smoky pewter, desaturated moss, aged silver, tiny ember warmth. The scene must be visible and richly atmospheric, not crushed blacks. Composition: open mist and sky across left half for interface text, dramatic silhouette at right, gentle calm lower foreground for UI. Wide 16:9. Environment ONLY, no text, lettering, logos, interface, icons, borders, watermarks.

The folder objects and illustrative project studies are CSS artwork. The gallery study does not reproduce Ruthie’s paintings, and the TileRecall study is a conceptual illustration rather than an application screenshot.
