# Wallpaper artwork

Created and edited with the built-in image generation tool. The four scenes use small architectural Latin crosses, with thin wood, narrow crossbars above the midpoint, and shaded edges that blend with the surrounding architecture. The Keep city cross sits on a skinny pole and uses shaded blue wood. The Keep city bedroom window has a clearer seated laptop silhouette.

The source scenes before these cross refinements are preserved in commit `8acd0e1`. The selected assets and final prompts are listed below. Generated originals remain in the image library.
