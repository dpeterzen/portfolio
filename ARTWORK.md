# Office wallpaper artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final PNG assets are stored in the repository. Earlier landscape explorations were replaced by these office scenes.

## Dune office

`dist/assets/dune-office.png`

Pixel art operating system wallpaper, wide 16:9. A dark cozy lo-fi office nook inside a futuristic desert outpost, viewed as a side-on three-quarter room scene like a 1990s point-and-click adventure. Right half: one small person seen from behind sitting in a chair using a glowing LAPTOP at a compact wooden desk tucked into the corner where two adobe walls meet. Face not visible. Clearly recognizable desk lamp, mug, stack of books, narrow wall shelf, cables, one square radio, simple ventilation duct. A large rectangular window above the desk shows blocky desert settlement buildings with antennae and distant mesas at dusk. Warm low light; muted dusty brown, taupe, ochre, dark sand palette with 8-12 closely related tones, laptop screen soft sand cream. Enough detail to identify buildings and individual objects, but restrained large flat pixel color clusters, not intricate textures. Visible chunky 320x180-style pixel grid, crisp nearest-neighbor edges, deliberate sparse dithering only at shadows. The LEFT 40 PERCENT of the image is a quiet dark adobe wall and floor with few details, providing negative space for desktop icons and a small window overlay. Office nook and person occupy lower right, window/settlement upper right. Relaxed, solitary, slightly hacker bedroom mood, no photorealism, no smooth digital painting, no rich multicolor palette, no typography, no logos, no UI, no watermarks.

## Keep office

`dist/assets/keep-office.png`

Pixel art operating system wallpaper, wide 16:9. A dark cozy lo-fi office nook inside a mythical knight's medieval castle, viewed as a side-on three-quarter room scene like a 1990s point-and-click adventure. Right half: one small person seen from behind sitting in a chair using a glowing LAPTOP at a compact wooden desk tucked into the corner where two stone walls meet. Face not visible. Clearly recognizable desk lamp, mug, stack of books, narrow wall shelf, a hanging simple sword and small shield, a book on the floor. Large arched window above the desk shows clearly recognizable battlement buildings and castle towers under a small moon. Muted slate-blue, ink, blue-gray palette with 8-12 closely related tones; pale laptop glow and a tiny dim lamplight highlight in cool pewter. Enough detail to identify the castle buildings and individual room objects, but restrained large flat pixel color clusters, not intricate brick textures. Visible chunky 320x180-style pixel grid, crisp nearest-neighbor edges, deliberate sparse dithering only at shadows. The LEFT 40 PERCENT of the image is a quiet dark stone wall and floor with very few details, providing negative space for desktop icons and a small window overlay. Office nook and person occupy lower right, window/castle upper right. Mysterious, relaxed, solitary, old computer hacker in a fantasy castle mood. No photorealism, no smooth digital painting, no rich multicolor palette, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks.

The pixel icons and project previews are code-native SVG and CSS. The preview of TileRecall is conceptual; the gallery preview does not reproduce Ruthie’s paintings. The seated figures are stylized, rear-facing representations, not portraits based on a reference photo.
