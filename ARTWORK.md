# Office wallpaper artwork

Edited with the built-in image generation tool, using the previous office scenes as references. These wide compositions make the laptop user much smaller within each room and simplify the scenes into chunky pixel forms and restrained tonal palettes.

## Dune office — wide view

`dist/assets/dune-office-wide.png`

Redraw this reference as a MUCH MORE DISTANT, simplified lo-fi PIXEL ART desktop wallpaper. Keep its idea: a person working on a laptop in the corner of a desert outpost building, with desert buildings outside. Make an EXTREME WIDE SHOT from across a large room, camera pulled back at least 4x: the entire person is a tiny sprite ONLY 6–8% OF THE IMAGE HEIGHT; the desk is ONLY 12–15% OF THE IMAGE WIDTH. Put this tiny desk nook at about 78% across and 64% down. Show much more surrounding room, large structural walls, floor and a small rectangular window looking toward blocky desert buildings. Laptop, simple desk lamp, one shelf and 2 or 3 books remain recognizable at sprite scale. STYLE IS CRITICAL: truly low-resolution early-1990s game art drawn on a 240x135 pixel grid and enlarged nearest-neighbor; chunky hard stair-stepped pixels, cartoon-simple geometry. Use ONLY 7 flat solid colors in a tightly unified dark ochre/taupe/sand family: deep brown shadows, medium brown walls, dusty tan building silhouettes, small warm sand laptop highlight. Solid flat fills, absolutely NO gradients, NO shading noise, NO texture, NO dithering, NO painterly realism, NO soft glow, no detailed brickwork. Dark relaxed atmosphere, broad quiet dark wall area on left 45% for desktop UI. The environment and architecture dominate; the person is a tiny distant detail. Wide 16:9. No writing, letters, interface, logo, watermark. This must look radically simpler and much more zoomed-out than the reference.
